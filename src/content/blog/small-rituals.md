I like routines that leave room for detours. Same café, different seat. Same walk, but take the left turn when the light looks interesting.

The best afternoons this year started as *I'll just pop out for ten minutes.*

- Keep the ritual.
- Drop the schedule.
- Follow whatever catches your eye.
